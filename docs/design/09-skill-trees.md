# Skill trees

Each job has 3 branches. Each branch has 5 skills, and the fifth is a capstone. The trees lean on what makes this game different from other RPGs: the citizen, toggles, Discipline, wages, corpses and element fields.

## Rules

Based on Diablo 2 and rAthena.

- A merc gets 1 skill point per level after level 1.
- A normal run ends around level 25, so a merc has about 24 points. Maxing every skill is not possible.
- Skills have up to 5 ranks. Capstones have 1 rank.
- Each tier unlocks at a merc level, as in Diablo 2.

| Tier | Merc level |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 8 |
| 4 | 12 |
| Capstone | 16 |

- A skill needs at least 1 rank in the skill before it in the same branch, as rAthena's prerequisites.
- Some skills have synergies, as in Diablo 2. Each rank in the named skill raises the other skill's effect.
- Recruits arrive with their points unspent.
- A trainer in a Town resets a merc's points for gold.

In the lists below, **A** marks an active skill and **P** marks a passive.

## In the game so far

- **Experience:** each kill is worth 10 per monster level, 35 for an elite and 150 for a boss. It is shared evenly by the living mercs. A level needs `20 × level^1.5` experience: 224 from level 5, 632 from level 10.
- **Points:** each level gives 1 skill point and 3 stat points. Recruits arrive with one skill point per level past the first, unspent, and nothing learned.
- **Learning:** open a merc (click it, or 1 and 2), then the Skills tab. A node needs its tier's level and a rank in the node before it in its branch. Skills have 5 ranks; capstones have 1.
- **Stats:** the Stats tab spends stat points on the six base stats and shows experience to the next level.
- A merc with points to spend shows a + on its party card. A level up shows a gold ring and rising light.

- Pin Down pins any enemy for now. It will also ground flyers once flyers exist.
- Ground skills leave a zone: a ring in the element's color, with a banner, rod, decoy or wall at its middle. Zones are `src/sim/zone.ts`.
- A merc with a healing skill (Heal, Renew, Resurrection, Sacred Ground) keeps 25 mana back for them, and spends only the rest on its other skills.
- No monster shoots yet, so Bastion, Rampart and Veil have nothing to stop. They work against ranged hits as soon as archers exist.
- Not yet: Consecrate keeping corpses from being raised, and Purge lifting corruption at each phase start, wait for those systems.

## Starting jobs

### Sellsword

Neutral. A cheap generalist who fights for money.

**Hired Blade**: plain weapon work.

1. Cleave (A): hits up to 3 enemies in the front arc.
2. Second Wind (P): once per stage, at 30% health, heals 10% to 30% of max health.
3. Rush (A): charges an enemy that is attacking the citizen and adds stun buildup.
4. Weapon Habit (P): +3% attack speed per rank with the weapon type it has used longest this run.
5. Veteran of Many Wars (P, capstone): gains +1 rank in a random learned skill for each phase it survives, up to +3.

**Coin**: skills that use gold.

1. Hazard Pay (P): if the player pays more than the wage at the start of a phase, +2% damage per rank for each 10% extra, for that phase.
2. Pocket Change (P): enemies it kills drop extra gold.
3. Bribe (A): costs gold and mana. Makes a non-elite Human enemy leave the fight.
4. Haggler (P): Town prices drop by 3% per rank while it is in the party.
5. Paid in Full (P, capstone): if paid double its wage at the start of a phase, +1 rank to every learned skill for that phase.

**Dirty Tricks**: fights without honor.

1. Pocket Sand (A): a cone that adds blind buildup.
2. Hamstring (A): a hit that slows the target.
3. Low Blow (P): +crit rate against blinded or slowed targets. Synergy: Pocket Sand.
4. Play Dead (P): the first time it would die in a stage, it lies still for 3 seconds instead, then gets up with 20% health. Monsters ignore it while it lies still.
5. Cutpurse (P, capstone): each hit has a chance to steal one buff from the target and keep it.

### Vanguard

Earth. The tank.

**Bulwark**: shields and taunts.

1. Challenge (A): taunts every enemy within 3 tiles.
2. Shield Wall (A): blocks every hit from the front for a few seconds. It cannot move during it.
3. Bastion (P): allies standing behind it take less ranged damage.
4. Rampart (A): plants its shield to make a short wall that stops ranged physical hits. Based on rAthena's Pneuma.
5. Immovable (P, capstone): cannot be knocked back, stunned or feared during Shield Wall or the Hold stance.

**Breaker**: breaks armor and enemies.

1. Sunder (P): melee hits lower the target's armor, stacking.
2. Crush (A): a heavy hit with stagger and Earth buildup.
3. Shield Bash (A): stun buildup. Interrupts casts.
4. Fault Line (A): a shockwave in a line that adds Earth buildup. Synergy: Crush.
5. Earthshaker (P, capstone): when an enemy near it becomes Petrified, every enemy within 3 tiles of that enemy gets Earth buildup.

**Oathkeeper**: guards the citizen.

1. Vow (P): +armor while within 4 tiles of the citizen.
2. Intercept (A): dashes between the citizen and the nearest threat.
3. Shared Burden (P): takes 5% per rank of the damage the citizen would take.
4. Banner (A): plants a banner. Allies near it gain morale and resist fear.
5. Last Line (P, capstone): when the citizen drops below 30% health, taunts every enemy within 5 tiles of the citizen. Once per stage.

### Warden

Neutral. The bodyguard.

**Shadow**: stays close and takes the hits.

1. Interpose (P): takes hits aimed at the citizen, at 70% of their damage, while within 2 tiles.
2. Tether (P): moves faster toward the citizen. Never chases more than 3 tiles away from the citizen.
3. Retaliate (P): attacks anyone in reach who damages the citizen, for free.
4. Cover (A): pulls the citizen behind it. For 3 seconds, projectiles from the front cannot reach the citizen.
5. Living Shield (P, capstone): Interpose takes 40% instead of 70%, and the Warden gains a Ward equal to the damage it took.

**Watchman**: sees threats before they arrive.

1. Sentry (P): reveals hidden and invisible enemies within 6 tiles of the citizen.
2. Tripwire (A): sets an alarm trap that marks and slows the enemy that triggers it.
3. Read the Room (P): reveals disguised enemies, such as the assassin refugee, at the start of a stage.
4. Night Eyes (P): removes night penalties for the party. Widens the lantern's light.
5. Never Surprised (P, capstone): Ambush nodes show on the map. In ambushes, enemies start stunned for 1 second.

**Executioner**: punishes anyone who touches the citizen.

1. Marked for Death (P): an enemy that damages the citizen is marked for 6 seconds. Every merc deals 5% more damage per rank to marked enemies.
2. Pursue (A): leaps to a marked enemy.
3. Debt Collector (P): +crit rate against marked enemies.
4. Execute (A): kills a marked non-boss enemy below 20% health.
5. Blood Debt (P, capstone): killing a marked enemy heals the citizen for 10% of the enemy's max health.

### Duelist

Neutral. Daggers and swords.

**Blade**: fast hits on one target.

1. Lunge (A): a thrust that closes distance.
2. Riposte (P): chance to parry melee hits. A parry triggers a free counter.
3. Flurry (A): four quick hits. Each one goes through the full damage pipeline.
4. Bleed Out (P): crits add bleed, which deals True damage over time.
5. Thousand Cuts (P, capstone): each hit in a row on the same target adds 3% damage. Switching targets resets it. This rewards a strict target toggle.

**Showmanship**: fights for an audience.

1. Flourish (P): kills restore mana and morale.
2. Taunt the Proud (A): taunts elites only.
3. Duel (A): binds the Duelist and one enemy for 6 seconds. Neither can target or be targeted by anyone else. This takes an elite out of the fight.
4. Crowd Pleaser (P): during a Duel, the party's morale rises.
5. Grand Duel (P, capstone): Duel lasts until one of them dies. The Duelist gets +50% crit rate during it.

**Footwork**: never stands still.

1. Sidestep (P): +evasion.
2. Feint (A): the next attack cannot be blocked.
3. Backstab (P): +damage from behind. Facing matters.
4. Shadowstep (A): teleports behind a caster or healer.
5. Untouchable (P, capstone): after an evade, the next hit is a crit.

### Ranger

Storm. Bows.

**Hawkeye**: picks off priority targets.

1. Aimed Shot (A): a long windup for a high damage shot.
2. Pin Down (A): grounds a flyer for 4 seconds, so melee mercs can reach it.
3. Eagle Eye (P): +range. +accuracy against flyers.
4. Piercing Arrow (A): hits every enemy in a line.
5. Killshot (P, capstone): the first shot at an enemy that has not noticed the party is a crit at double damage.

**Trapper**: guards the path behind the citizen.

1. Snare (A): a trap that roots.
2. Rearguard (P): sets traps on the tiles the citizen walked over in the last 5 seconds. This protects the citizen's back.
3. Bait (A): places a decoy that draws monsters for a few seconds.
4. Spike Field (A): an area that slows and adds bleed.
5. Killing Ground (P, capstone): when the citizen shouts Hold, the Ranger sets a ring of traps around the Hold point.

**Stormcaller**: arrows charged with Storm.

1. Charged Arrow (A): a Storm arrow that chains to 1 more target.
2. Static (P): +Storm buildup.
3. Arc Volley (A): several arrows. Each one chains.
4. Lightning Rod (A): plants a rod that makes a Storm field. Based on rAthena's Violent Gale.
5. Thunderhead (P, capstone): when an enemy is Shocked, Shock chains to every other Shocked enemy within 6 tiles.

### Arcanist

Fire and Frost. Area magic.

**Pyromancy**: Fire.

1. Firebolt (A): a Fire projectile.
2. Ignite (P): +Burn buildup.
3. Fireball (A): a Fire area. No friendly fire. Synergy: Firebolt.
4. Volcano (A): makes a Fire field. Allies inside deal more Fire damage. Based on rAthena's Volcano.
5. Inferno (A, capstone): a very large Fire area with friendly fire. The strongest attack in the tree.

**Cryomancy**: Frost.

1. Frost Shard (A): a Frost projectile.
2. Chill (P): Frost hits slow the target.
3. Frost Nova (A): a ring of Frost around the Arcanist that adds Freeze buildup.
4. Glacial Wall (A): a wall of ice that blocks a path for a few seconds. Good for closing a choke point near the citizen.
5. Shatterpoint (P, capstone): an enemy killed while Frozen shatters and deals Frost damage to enemies around it. It leaves no corpse.

**Weave**: control of the mana itself.

1. Mana Flow (P): +mana regeneration.
2. Overchannel (P): when out of mana, spells cost health instead.
3. Elemental Sense (P): picks Fire or Frost for each target based on its current element. This makes the "weak to my element" toggle work better.
4. Echo (P): chance to cast the last spell again for free.
5. Controlled Burn (P, capstone): while the Arcanist's effective Discipline is 60 or more, its friendly fire spells spare allies and the citizen.

### Cleric

Radiant. Healing and protection.

**Mercy**: healing and resurrection.

1. Heal (A): heals one ally or the citizen.
2. Renew (A): heals over time.
3. Cleanse (A): removes harmful statuses and clears buildup.
4. Resurrection (A): brings a dead merc back with 10% to 50% health. A long windup. Based on rAthena's Resurrection.
5. Martyr (P, capstone): when the Cleric dies, every dead ally within 5 tiles is resurrected at 20% health.

**Sanctuary**: barriers.

1. Ward (A): gives an ally or the citizen a pool that absorbs magical damage.
2. Sacred Ground (A): an area that heals over time. Based on rAthena's Sanctuary.
3. Wall of Faith (A): a tile that stops a number of melee hits. Best cast on the citizen's tile. Based on rAthena's Safety Wall.
4. Veil (A): an area that stops ranged physical hits. Based on rAthena's Pneuma.
5. Aegis (P, capstone): the citizen always has a Ward that refills outside combat.

**Inquisition**: fights the Occult and the dead.

1. Smite (A): Radiant damage.
2. Holy Light (P): +Sear buildup.
3. Turn Undead (A): chance to destroy a non-boss Undead at once. Based on rAthena's Turn Undead.
4. Consecrate (A): a Radiant field. Corpses inside cannot be raised.
5. Purge (P, capstone): Occult enemies take 25% more damage from the party. At each phase start, removes corruption from every merc.

These jobs do not appear in the first pick of a run. They are outlines. Full trees come later.

## Design notes

- Every job has at least one skill that cares about the citizen. The citizen is the center of every fight.
- Some capstones change how a toggle works instead of adding numbers. Examples: Thousand Cuts, Elemental Sense, Controlled Burn.
- Corpses matter: Resurrection, Martyr, Consecrate, Shatterpoint and the Necromancer all use them.
- Money matters: the Sellsword's Coin branch ties wages to power.
- Any active skill can also appear on an autocast card. See [cards](06-items-inventory.md#cards).
