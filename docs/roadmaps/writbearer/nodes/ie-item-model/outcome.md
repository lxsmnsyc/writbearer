# Outcome: ie-item-model

## Built

- `Tier` (const enum Normal, Exceptional, Elite), `Requirements`, `ItemBase.tier` and `ItemBase.requirements` in `src/sim/item.ts`, with `meetsRequirements(item, unit)`, `requirementText(item)` and `checksRequirements(item)` (weapon, shield, accessory).
- `src/content/items.ts`: STR/DEX requirements on the Normal weapon, shield, helm and body bases; `TIERS` (Normal id to `{ exceptional, elite }` siblings, built by `makeSibling`) and `TIER_BASES`. Siblings are `<id>Exceptional` / `<id>Elite`, named "Tempered X" / "Masterwork X", damage and armor x2.2 / x3, requirement +8 / +16 on the Normal base's stat (DEX for weapons, STR for armor when it had none). `BASES` is unchanged; `BASE_BY_ID` includes the siblings, so `loadItem` resolves them.
- `src/gen/loot.ts`: `tierBase(base, itemLevel)` runs after `rng.pick` in `rollItem` (`EXCEPTIONAL_FROM = 12`, `ELITE_FROM = 24`). No RNG draw. `itemLines` adds the "Requires STR n, DEX n" line.
- `equipFromPack` (`src/game/inventory.ts`) refuses weapon, shield and accessory with a message when unmet. Head, Body, Cloak and Boots always equip. `Unit.equip`, `spawnMerc` and save loading are unchanged and do not check.
- UI: unmet items grey out in `src/ui/inventory.tsx`; the card shows the requirement line red for the chosen merc (`unit` prop on `ItemSlot` and `ItemCard`).
- `test/sim/item-tiers.test.ts`.

## Deviations

- `src/ui/items.tsx` edited (not listed): an optional `unit` prop on `ItemSlot` and `ItemCard` so the tooltip can color the requirement line.
- Siblings are generated by code, not written out as data.
- No `upgrades` field on `ItemBase`; the link is the `TIERS` map.
- Scale factors are higher than first planned (2.2 and 3) so the rolled armor of every sibling clears the Normal max.

## Stubs left

- None. Accessories have no requirements and no tiers yet.

## Gotchas

- `ie-item-carry` reads `item.base.requirements?.str`. Tier siblings of armor ask for STR (Normal body armor: jerkin 4, hauberk 12).
- Thresholds 12 and 24 are above every item level the balance and boss tests reach, so their numbers did not move.
- A merc's `base.str` and `base.dex` are the job's template values; at Elite most mercs cannot wield a weapon until stat gear or shards raise them.
- New bases must stay out of `BASES` (loot indexes it with `rng.pick`).

## Proposed learnings

- Stronger tiers of a base are swapped in by item level after the pick, never by an extra RNG draw | seed-sensitive tests pin the draw sequence | src/gen | yes (the draw sequence test)
