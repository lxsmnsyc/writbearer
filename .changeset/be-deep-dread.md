Dread and the unseen: the Drowned Bell, a stationary bell that rings Dread across 6 tiles while other monsters stand near it; the Whisperer, a frail Hollowed that builds Dread within 4 tiles; and the Skulker, a hidden Hollowed that shows itself 2 tiles from the bearer, strikes and hides again. Each has a figure, a card, a bestiary entry, a compendium slot and a pack.
