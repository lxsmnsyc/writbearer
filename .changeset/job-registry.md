The Vanguard, Ranger and Cleric now know their four spells and their innate passive from level 1, and raise them with shards. The skill trees are gone. Old saves give back their skill points as shards. Each rank of a spell now adds half as much as a tree rank did, as spells go to rank 10; Heal keeps its old gain. Until their elite upgrades are built, the three jobs lose the tree skills that became upgrades, such as Vow, Bait and Renew.
