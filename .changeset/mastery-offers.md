Mercs earn elite upgrades in a run: each Waystation from the second phase on offers every living merc 3 upgrades of its own spells, and a new Mastery road reward offers 3 cards for 3 different mercs. A merc with no upgrade left is offered +2 shards. An open offer is kept in the saved run. A run saved mid-layer may see some fights ahead turn into Mastery roads after the update.
