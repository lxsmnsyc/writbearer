# writbearer

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 4769d34: The Arcanist is a real job: Frost Nova, Fireball, Frost Shard and Firebolt, the innate Elemental Sense that picks the bolt a target is weaker to, and its own look. It is not offered at the Guild House yet.
- d41d2c5: The Arcanist's 12 elite upgrades work: Glacial Wall, Rimefield, Cold Snap, Volcano, Inferno, Echo, Chill, Shatterpoint, Overchannel, Ignite, Mana Flow and Piercing Bolt.
- 706667f: Three new elite affixes: Storm Enchanted throws sparks at nearby mercs when hit, Mana Burn drains mana, and Teleport blinks next to the bearer. A Herald elite now runs for a horn post and blows it. The Horn of the Road has Teleport instead of Swift.
- bde6cb8: Raisers: a monster can stand a fallen monster up again as a weaker undead on its own side, after a long cast shown as a ring on the ground. A stun breaks the cast and the body stays. A torn body, one laid to rest and one that was itself raised cannot be raised. No monster raises yet.
- 62950ea: The Drowned Undercroft's Church keepers: the Ossuary Priest raises the fallen as husks, the Tithe-Taker steals one item an area, the Confessor strips a merc's weapon, and the Flagellant hits harder as it is hurt and makes mercs bleed. Each has a card, a bestiary entry, a compendium slot and packs in layer 2.
- 4736fcb: Dread and the unseen: the Drowned Bell, a stationary bell that rings Dread across 6 tiles while other monsters stand near it; the Whisperer, a frail Hollowed that builds Dread within 4 tiles; and the Skulker, a hidden Hollowed that shows itself 2 tiles from the bearer, strikes and hides again. Each has a figure, a card, a bestiary entry, a compendium slot and a pack.
- bbd6e53: The Crown's last guard: the Revenant Knight, a slow armored Spectral ghost that shrugs off plain blows, and the Gilded Effigy, a golden statue that wakes only when the bearer comes close and cannot be stunned or frightened. Each has a card, a bestiary entry, a compendium slot and a pack in layer 3.
- ef8b19e: Flyers: a monster can fly. While aloft, melee mercs cannot pick it; ranged attacks, spells and arrows can. It lands to strike and perches now and then, so a melee party is never stuck. Pin Down grounds a flyer for the root's time, and Eagle Eye never misses one. No monster flies yet.
- 88b0a88: Kidnappers can grab a ward (the citizen of an Escort) and drag it to an exit; a ward that gets there is lost. A stun, root, fear or kill on the kidnapper lets the ward go where it stands. No monster is a kidnapper yet.
- a0bd526: Strippers: a monster can disable one item on a merc until the area ends or the bearer hands it back. The item stays in its slot but gives no bonus; a stripped weapon leaves the merc bare-handed. No monster uses it yet.
- 768bd72: Thieves can take an item from the pack, or a share of the gold when the pack is empty, and run for an exit. A thief killed on the way drops what it took; one that reaches the exit is gone with it. No monster is a thief yet.
- 08016a5: Each layer rolls its boss from a designed pool of three (all twelve in endless), and the layer map names the boss. The four current bosses are registered as boss defs.
- 559c95a: Mind control: a controlled merc fights for the monsters for 6 seconds, then returns to its side, hunt, orders and target. The bearer cannot be controlled, Cleanse lifts it, and a controlled merc counts for nothing in party wipe, morale, wages or kills. Each merc's last released skill is recorded so a boss can cast a copy at that rank for no mana.
- 059277f: Mercs can learn four spells, raise them with a shard gained each level, and give each spell one elite upgrade, shown in a new spell panel.
- f578a95: The Cleric's 12 elite upgrades work: Cleanse, Wall of Faith, Twin Ward, Renew, Resurrection, Martyr, Veil, Consecrate, Pilgrim's Rest, Turn Undead, Purge and Holy Light. The Cleric no longer sees an elite that a Duelist is dueling.
- 0abae42: Bodies can be laid to rest. One rule, `canRaise`, decides whether a dead merc can be raised: torn, rested or guarded bodies stay dead, in resurrection, the Temple and the Creditor's reveal.
- d81557c: The Duelist's 12 elite upgrades work: Grand Duel, Crowd Pleaser, Taunt the Proud, Flourish, Smoke Step, Assassin's Mark, Feint, Sidestep, Untouchable, Bleed Out, Thousand Cuts and Whirlwind.
- b26b3a8: The Duelist is a real job: Duel, Shadowstep, Lunge and Flurry, the innate Riposte, and its own look. It is not offered at the Guild House yet.
- 96ba75c: A unit's defense element can change for a while. Skills can shift it, gear can carry one, and Freeze and Petrify now layer with them, so overlapping effects end in any order and leave the right element.
- 33f3f56: Some Escorts now hide an assassin among the refugees. It walks with them and looks like one, then turns on the bearer. Hidden units can now exist: mercs ignore them until they strike or are hit.
- 586dbf3: Items and cards can set the wearer's defense element and raise one of their spells by a rank. The tooltip shows "Defense: Fire 2" and "+1 to Heal". Two example cards, the Ember Gem and the Healing Card, use them.
- 7565044: Weapons, shields and armor come in three tiers, Normal, Tempered and Masterwork, and the stronger ones drop at item level 12 and 24. Weapons, shields and accessories ask for STR or DEX: a merc below it cannot wield the item, and the tooltip shows the requirement in red.
- 98ad696: Mercs can now be told to go for enemies weak to their element, a new choice in the target priority list.
- 8659166: The Vanguard, Ranger and Cleric now know their four spells and their innate passive from level 1, and raise them with shards. The skill trees are gone. Old saves give back their skill points as shards. Each rank of a spell now adds half as much as a tree rank did, as spells go to rank 10; Heal keeps its old gain. Until their elite upgrades are built, the three jobs lose the tree skills that became upgrades, such as Vow, Bait and Renew.
- c6f51ad: Mercs earn elite upgrades in a run: each Waystation from the second phase on offers every living merc 3 upgrades of its own spells, and a new Mastery road reward offers 3 cards for 3 different mercs. A merc with no upgrade left is offered +2 shards. An open offer is kept in the saved run. A run saved mid-layer may see some fights ahead turn into Mastery roads after the update.
- e6a62c0: The Ranger's 12 elite upgrades work: Rearguard, Bait, Spike Field, Killing Ground, Storm Cell, Overcharge, Pin Down, Eagle Eye, Killshot, Arc Volley, Thunderhead and Piercing Arrow. Its spells rank to 10, and Charged Arrow cools down in 3.5 seconds, not 4.
- dbab1d4: The Guild House offers five recruits, drawn by slot: a guard, a striker, a support and two more, five different jobs. The Sellsword, Warden, Duelist and Arcanist can now be hired, at the Guild House, from the deserter Event and by the fires of endless mode. The party still holds four mercs at most. The Warden's Interpose now covers the bearer from 3 tiles and takes 80% of the hit, and the Warden chases nothing farther than 4 tiles from the bearer. A Storm arrow's chain now hits for 1.5 times the arrow's damage. The merc sheet shows the chase distance after the job's limit.
- 605fc81: The Sellsword's 12 elite upgrades work: Shield Rush, Chain Rush, Knock Aside, Bribe, Play Dead, Salt the Wound, Low Blow, Cutpurse, Cripple, Weapon Habit, Veteran of Many Wars and Pocket Change.
- 3d72ee6: The Sellsword is a real job: Rush, Pocket Sand, Hamstring and Cleave, the innate Second Wind, and its own look. It is not offered at the Guild House yet.
- 857d151: The Vanguard's 12 elite upgrades work: Vow, Shared Burden, Charge, Last Line, Banner, Shield Bash, Immovable, Rampart, Bastion, Fault Line, Earthshaker and Sundering Blow. Challenge and Fault Line no longer reach an enemy dueled by a Duelist.
- 3d668e4: The Warden has its four spells (Cover, Execute, Tripwire, Pursue), the Interpose innate that marks enemies who hit the bearer, and a lantern look. It is not offered at the Guild House yet.
- ea4925c: The Warden's 12 elite upgrades work: Living Shield, Tether, Retaliate, Blood Debt, Hangman's Reach, Last Rites, Sentry, Trap Field, Never Surprised, Marked for Death, Debt Collector and Read the Room. With Never Surprised, Ambushes show on the map.
